## Observed cause and resulting behavior

The shared unpaid guard rejected every catalog snapshot, coupon and discount
before the modal opened. The old save code also recomputed gross product totals
without retaining the discount. Merely removing the guard would lose money terms.
The guard regression reproduced the reported failure against the original code.

The price-aware controller now opens discounted ordinary invoices. Owners can
replace existing products, change positive integer quantities and choose existing
catalog discounts or a custom per-unit discount only when their effective policy
allows it. Product prices come from WooCommerce and discounts from the existing
sales-catalog engine, including the seller/manager policy. Example: 2 units at
1,000 toman minus 100 toman per unit produce a total of 1,800 toman.

Changing only payment terms preserves the issued item prices, discount snapshot
and net amount even when today's product price or discount policy differs.
Owners can explicitly select «محاسبه با قیمت و تخفیف مجاز فعلی محصولات» to use
current prices without changing the SKU. Every changed input invalidates the
previous preview. Save requires a new server-calculated, signed preview.

The last sender may edit permitted payment terms but cannot forge product,
quantity, discount or price-refresh changes unless also the seller owner. The
existing full-to-partial restriction remains exclusive to the last sender.
The first-stage amount must be positive and below the net invoice amount.
Persian/Arabic digits and hundredths are supported for money.

## Integrity and compatibility

- Existing action `sn_invoice_pre_payment_edit` now uses read → preview → save;
  see [API contract](../API_CONTRACTS.md). Cached older clients cannot save without
  this handshake. Both shell enqueues use a content hash, including fixed-time ZIPs.
- Preview/save lock invoice, initial stage and item rows. Preview rolls back.
  Save recalculates under lock; stale revisions, expired/forged previews, changed
  prices and revoked discount grants fail without partial writes.
- Repricing atomically updates existing invoice totals/discount snapshot, existing
  items and first-stage amount. A forced stage-write failure rolled everything back.
  Amount-only edits retain original pricing fields and items.
- No DDL, new table, migration, role, capability or hierarchy-policy change.
  No new customer/public endpoint, token, gateway or callback contract. Invoice
  id/code/access token remain unchanged; none is regenerated by this action.
- No payment-ledger row, wallet credit, commission, order fulfillment, SMS or
  external payment call is created by this operation. Existing activity logging
  records whether the quote was repriced and its discount total.
- Paid, approved, archived, cancelled, receipt/manual-payment, financial-review,
  active gateway-request, later-stage and fulfilled invoices stay locked. Legacy
  final aliases and inferred prior payment also prevent unsafe editing.
- The shared support-console caller keeps its default discount block; it cannot
  use this new price-aware bypass. Subscription+ keeps its specialized editor;
  conversion/assessment restrictions remain in their existing workflows.

## Explicit limits

Existing coupon, wheel reward, free-gift or unsnapshotted legacy discounts are
preserved when changing payment terms. Product/discount repricing for those
legacy benefits is refused with an explanation: their dedicated benefit engine
has not been integrated here. It is not safe to silently erase or guess them.
The number of existing item rows stays fixed. Primary customer identity/address,
paid invoices, bulk repair and creation/deletion of invoice lines are outside
this action. The browser fixture exercises the real modal and AJAX, not every
complete seller/manager panel. Production parity and the full supported PHP/WP
version matrix remain UNVERIFIED.
